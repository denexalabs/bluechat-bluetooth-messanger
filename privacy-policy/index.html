Privacy Policy

Bluechat-Bluetooth Messenger
Effective Date: July 16, 2026

DenexaLabs ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Bluetooth Messenger collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application. By using the application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About Bluechat-Bluetooth Messenger

Bluechat-Bluetooth Messenger is an offline communication application that enables nearby devices to exchange messages using Bluetooth technology. The application is designed to operate without requiring user registration or cloud-based messaging services.

2. Information We Collect

Bluechat-Bluetooth Messenger is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require users to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Government identification
  • Payment information
  • Contacts

3. Device Permissions

The application may request certain Android permissions solely to provide its core functionality.

Permission Purpose
Bluetooth Discover and communicate with nearby Bluetooth devices.
Nearby Devices Required on newer Android versions for Bluetooth communication.
Location (GPS) Some Android versions require location permission for Bluetooth discovery. Location is processed only on your device and is not transmitted to DenexaLabs.
Notifications Display message notifications.

4. Messages and Communication

Messages exchanged through Bluechat-Bluetooth Messenger are transmitted directly between nearby devices.

  • Messages are not uploaded to our servers.
  • We cannot read your conversations.
  • We do not monitor message content.
  • Your conversations remain stored only on your own device unless you choose to delete them.

5. Location Information

If location permission is granted, it is used only for Bluetooth functionality or features that rely on device location. The application does not share your location with other users and does not upload your location to our servers.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or disclose your personal information to advertisers, marketing companies, or other third parties.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information stored on your device. While we strive to protect your data, no electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Bluechat-Bluetooth Messenger is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

The current version of Bluechat-Bluetooth Messenger does not use cloud storage, advertising services, analytics platforms, or other third-party services that collect personal information on our behalf.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
